Upload CSV or Excel (.xlsx), review the preview, then confirm. ISBN is used to safely match a title on re-import. If a book has no ISBN, enter a stable, unique Catalogue ID (for example LIB-LOCAL-001) so later imports update that same record. Total quantity is the complete shelf quantity; active loans are preserved and cannot be made greater than the imported total.
